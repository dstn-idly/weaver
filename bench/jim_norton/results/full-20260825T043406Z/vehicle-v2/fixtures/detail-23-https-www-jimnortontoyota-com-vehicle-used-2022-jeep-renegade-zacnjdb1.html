    Vehicle Description

    Discover the remarkable 2022 Jeep Renegade Latitude, a compact SUV that blends style, capability, and convenience. With its sleek exterior and well-appointed interior, this Renegade is ready to elevate your driving experience.

    - Custom Features: This Renegade Latitude is a local trade-in that has never been a rental vehicle, ensuring a clean history and reliable performance.

    - Standout Features:
    - Convenience Group with features like a Windshield Wiper De-Icer, 115V Auxiliary Power Outlet, Rain Sensitive Windshield Wipers, and more
    - Heated Front Seats and Heated Steering Wheel for comfortable driving in any weather
    - Power Adjust 8-Way Driver Seat and Power 2-Way Driver Lumbar Adjust for optimal support
    - 40/20/40 Rear Seat with Trunk Pass-Thru for versatile cargo carrying

    The Renegade Latitude's 1.3L I4 engine paired with a 9-Speed 948TE Automatic transmission and 4WD delivers an impressive blend of efficiency and capability, earning an EPA-estimated 23 city / 29 highway MPG. Whether you're navigating city streets or venturing off the beaten path, this Jeep is up for the challenge.

    Inside, the Renegade Latitude surrounds you with premium features like the Uconnect 4C Nav system with an 8.4" display, Apple CarPlay/Android Auto integration, and a ParkView Rear Back-Up Camera for added confidence. The comfortable, spacious cabin and thoughtful storage solutions make this Jeep an ideal companion for your daily commute or weekend adventures.

    Experience the perfect balance of style, capability, and technology in the 2022 Jeep Renegade Latitude. Visit us today to take this impressive SUV for a test drive and discover why it should be your next vehicle.
