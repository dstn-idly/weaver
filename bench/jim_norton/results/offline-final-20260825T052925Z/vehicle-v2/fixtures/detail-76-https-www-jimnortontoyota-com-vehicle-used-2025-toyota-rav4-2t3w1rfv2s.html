    Vehicle Description

    This 2025 Toyota RAV4 XLE offers a practical and reliable foundation for your daily driving needs, combining efficient performance with thoughtful design.

    - Front Door Smart Key System w/Push Button Start
    - Tilt & Slide Moon Roof
    - Convenience Package with power liftgate and jam protection
    - Remote keyless entry
    - Front dual zone automatic temperature control
    - Power driver seat
    - Exterior parking camera
    - 17" alloy wheels
    - Rear window defroster
    - Steering wheel mounted audio controls
    - SiriusXM AM/FM audio system
    - Electronic stability control and traction control
    - Four-wheel independent suspension
    - Split folding rear seat

    The 2.5L four-cylinder engine delivers 27 miles per gallon in city driving and 35 on the highway, providing solid efficiency for your commute or weekend excursions. The eight-speed automatic transmission and front-wheel drive ensure smooth acceleration and reliable handling in various driving conditions. This local trade-in arrives well-maintained and ready for its next owner.

    The XLE trim includes a comprehensive suite of comfort and convenience features designed to make your time behind the wheel more enjoyable. The power liftgate with jam protection simplifies loading cargo, while the tilt and slide moon roof adds natural light and a sense of openness to the cabin. Dual front zone climate control allows driver and passenger to set their preferred temperatures independently, keeping everyone comfortable regardless of the season.

    Safety remains a priority with this RAV4. The vehicle features an exterior parking camera for added awareness when reversing, electronic stability control for confident handling, and a comprehensive airbag system including front, side impact, and overhead protection. Toyota's Safety Connect system provides emergency communication capabilities, giving you additional peace of mind on the road.

    The cabin is organized with practical storage throughout, including front and rear door bins, front center armrest, and an overhead console. The split folding rear seat provides flexibility for your cargo needs, whether you're transporting passengers or equipment. Full power windows and mirrors, along with telescoping steering wheel adjustment, ensure you can customize your driving position to suit your preference.

    The interior features fabric seat trim with front bucket seats and a rear center armrest, creating a comfortable environment for daily driving. Illuminated entry lighting and vanity mirrors add convenience for nighttime visibility, while the trip computer and tachometer keep you informed of your vehicle's performance metrics.
